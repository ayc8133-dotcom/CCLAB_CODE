[ Space Sun-Eater ]

Scientific Name:

Solarphage

Discovered by / at:

Aric Chua, NYU Shanghai

Family:

Believed to descend from Helion, an ancient ancestral organism formed from the remnants of a dying star.

Habitat:

The deep space of Nebula void

Appearance:

This alien creature emits a soft glow with fluid, organic form structures. The skin gradient is made up varying shades of colours that create a nebula-like effect in outer space. There is constant restructuring of the body and movement as it emits energy.

Key Characteristics:

  • Glows in the darkness of space.
  • Absorbs light for energy source.
  • Adaptive color and movement.
  • Aesexual reproduction similar to mitosis.

What we know so far about Solarphage? Current observations suggest that Solarphage depends on light as its primary energy source. Whenever there is a presence of a light source around, they move towards them searching for energy. Upon exposure to light energy, Solarphages will absorb the energy leading to body growth until reaching a certain limit whereby it splits into two new organisms, allowing its population to expand. Without the presence of light energy, Solarphages tend to drift aimlessly within their habitat. Individual organisms display different movement speeds and colours, suggesting variation within the species.
Guidelines for human interaction with this creature As a researcher, you can use the mouse to move around the screen and guide the path of the light source while watching how the organisms react accordingly. The creatures will move towards the light, allowing you to observe how they search for energy. Once a Solarphage consumes the light making it disappear, a single click can revive the light source making it easier to continue observing them. Without light, the organisms drift around in space. When the population of Solarphage reaches a certain limit, you can press the spacebar to reset the population to one.
